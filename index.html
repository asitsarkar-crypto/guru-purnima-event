The gathering

Where nature and communities prospered together

Karmyog Vātika is a club for lifelong learning — a garden of shared practice where mindfulness, community, and the rhythms of nature meet. This Guru Purnima, the community gathered under the full moon for meditation, dharma katha, a yogic performance and a shared meal.

The evening was led by MahAcharya Shri Sourabh J. Sarkar, with special yogic performances by Acharya Rhea and Acharya Pritam. Scroll down to relive the evening in photos and the full recording.

How the evening unfolded

Four movements of the night

From the first walk into stillness to the shared meal that closed the evening.

6:00 – 6:45 PM

Prakriti Jagran Yatra

A gentle procession awakening awareness of nature — the opening walk that settled the mind before meditation began.

6:45 – 7:30 PM

Purnima Meditation & Dharma Katha

A guided full-moon meditation followed by dharma katha, led by MahAcharya Shri Sourabh J. Sarkar.

7:30 – 7:45 PM

Special Yogic Performance

A live yogic performance by Acharya Rhea and Acharya Pritam.

7:45 PM onwards

Maha Prasad

An evening meal shared together, offered to every attendee to close the gathering.
